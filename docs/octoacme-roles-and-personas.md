# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance/Testing Leads design and execute testing strategies to ensure software quality, reliability, and compliance. They work with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria and manage test coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Manage automated and manual testing efforts
- Identify, prioritize, and track defects
- Ensure test coverage meets quality standards
- Validate releases against quality gates
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable software
- Minimize production defects and regressions
- Reduce time spent on manual testing
- Ensure compliance with quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality metrics reporting
- Defect tracking and status updates
- Test case documentation and automation discussions

**Collaboration with Other Roles:**
- Works closely with Developers on test automation and test case design
- Partners with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Coordinates with Technical Leads on testing strategy and quality gates
- Supports Release/Deployment Managers in pre-release quality validation

---

## Stakeholder/Client Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Client Representatives serve as the voice of the business and end users. They define business requirements, validate solutions, and ensure delivery meets customer expectations and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Validate solutions meet customer needs
- Provide feedback on prototypes and deliverables
- Communicate business priorities and constraints
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT)
- Coordinate with product managers on scope and tradeoffs

### Goals
- Ensure delivery aligns with business objectives
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and ROI
- Reduce rework due to unclear requirements
- Enable informed decision-making on priorities

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and feedback sessions
- Regular stakeholder updates and demos
- UAT coordination and sign-offs

**Collaboration with Other Roles:**
- Partners with Product Managers on requirement validation and prioritization
- Engages with Project Managers on timeline and scope discussions
- Provides feedback to Developers through UAT and validation sessions
- Works with QA/Testing Leads on acceptance criteria and testing scenarios

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects define technical direction, make architectural decisions, and guide the technical strategy of projects. They balance innovation, quality, and maintainability while mentoring the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Make key technical decisions and trade-offs
- Review technical designs and code for architectural alignment
- Identify and manage technical risks and technical debt
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Evaluate and recommend tools and technologies

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Balance quality with delivery speed
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Develop engineering team capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review boards and design discussions
- Technical documentation and RFC (Request for Comments) reviews
- Code reviews focused on architectural alignment

**Collaboration with Other Roles:**
- Guides Developers on technical strategy and architectural decisions
- Works with Product Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Partners with Project Managers to identify technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Leads on testability and quality standards
- Supports Release/Deployment Managers on deployment architecture and infrastructure requirements

---

## Release/Deployment Manager

### Role Summary
Release/Deployment Managers coordinate release planning, oversee deployment execution, and manage production stability. They ensure releases are delivered safely and efficiently while minimizing disruption.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules
- Manage release notes and deployment documentation
- Coordinate with ops/infrastructure teams on deployment readiness
- Execute or oversee deployment processes
- Monitor deployments and manage rollback procedures
- Track deployment status and communicate outcomes
- Ensure compliance with deployment policies and standards

### Goals
- Execute reliable, timely releases
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Maintain clear visibility into release status
- Enable smooth transitions to production

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and release notes
- Deployment checklists and runbooks
- Post-deployment reviews and incident reports

**Collaboration with Other Roles:**
- Works with Project Managers on release timeline planning and dependency management
- Coordinates with Developers on deployment preparation and release readiness
- Partners with QA/Testing Leads on pre-release validation and quality gates
- Communicates with Technical Leads on deployment architecture and infrastructure needs
- Provides stakeholders with release status and deployment outcomes
